Why TravelMatch exists

Everyone wants to travel. Not everyone can find who to travel with.

Friends have different schedules. Budgets don't line up. Interests pull people toward different kinds of trips entirely. And going with strangers, without any sense of whether you'd actually get along, feels like a gamble most people aren't willing to take.

TravelMatch exists to make finding compatible travel companions a straightforward part of planning a trip — not a problem you solve by luck.

Compatibility over popularity

Safety & trust

Travel connections should feel safe

Meeting a travel companion through TravelMatch should never feel riskier than meeting one any other way. Some of this is already built in. Some of it is part of where we're headed.

    Email verification
